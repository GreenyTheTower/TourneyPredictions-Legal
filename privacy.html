TourneyPredictions Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 28, 2026

1. Introduction

TourneyPredictions is an independently operated Discord bot used to organize tournament prediction competitions for members of The Tower Discord community.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the bot collects, how it is used, and how users can request deletion of their information.

2. Information Collected

TourneyPredictions may store:

  • Discord user IDs and usernames.
  • Discord channel IDs.
  • Tournament predictions.
  • Tournament scores and Kudos.
  • Round and season participation history.
  • Tournament results and timestamps.

The bot does not require passwords, payment information, or personal contact information.

3. How Information Is Used

Collected information is used to operate prediction competitions, calculate results, maintain leaderboards, and preserve historical tournament statistics.

Information is not sold or used for advertising.

4. Data Storage

TourneyPredictions uses MongoDB Atlas for database storage and Render for application hosting.

These services process information as necessary to operate the bot.

5. Public Information

Tournament results and leaderboards may publicly display Discord usernames, predictions, scores, Kudos, and participation statistics.

When a participant submits or updates a prediction, the bot may publicly acknowledge the submission.

6. Data Retention

Tournament records may be retained across multiple seasons to support historical statistics.

Ending a season does not automatically delete previous results.

7. Data Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of their stored personal information, including predictions and associated tournament statistics.

To request deletion, contact ggreeny_ through Discord direct messages.

Requests will be reviewed and processed in accordance with applicable requirements. Deleting information may affect historical leaderboards.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information, including database authentication and restricted access to administrative credentials.

No online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot's functionality or data-handling practices change.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, data access requests, or deletion requests, contact:

Discord: ggreeny_

TourneyPredictions is independently operated and is not an official service of The Tower Discord server or its moderation team.
